# Delete your Hydro account

You can delete your **Hydro** account (by VISION SYSTEMS CORP) and its data at any time.

## Option 1 — In the app (instant)

1. Open **Hydro**.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes:
- your Firebase account (email, name, profile photo link);
- all app data on the device: water history, goals, streaks, badges, profile details and your profile photo.

## Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, send a request from the email address linked to your account:

- **To:** [CONTACT EMAIL]
- **Subject:** Delete my Hydro account

We will delete your account within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is deleted and what is kept

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Account (email, name, photo link) | Deleted permanently |
| Water history, goals, profile, badges | Deleted from the device (never stored on our servers) |
| Crash reports and anonymous analytics | Not linked to your account. Deleted automatically under Firebase retention settings |
